\label{cp:Recommendations}

{
  \parindent0pt


  To build on the current prototype and move towards a full-scale launch, we have outlined a clear roadmap for future work and development.

  \section{Immediate Next Steps (3-6 Months)}


  \begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Develop Custom Dataset}: Launch a data collection campaign to build a robust, annotated dataset of at least 5,000 images of local waste. This is the highest priority for improving model accuracy.

    \item  \textbf{Malicious Use and "Faking"}: Use the new dataset to fine-tune the Gemini Vision model to specialize in local trash identification and weight estimation.



    \item \textbf{Launch Pilot Program}: Roll out the Ayang web app to a limited number of pilot communities (e.g., 3-5 neighborhoods). This controlled launch will allow us to gather user feedback, identify bugs, and test our assumptions in a real-world environment.

    \item \textbf{Establish a Partnership}: Initiate a formal partnership with at least one local municipal waste authority or a private recycling company. This is critical to solving the "last mile" disposal problem and ensuring the sustainability of the effort.

  \end{itemize}

  \section{Medium-Term Goals (6-18 Months)}


  \begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Full Public Launch}: Based on the success of the pilot, launch Ayang to the general public in a target city. This will be accompanied by a marketing and outreach campaign to drive user adoption.

    \item \textbf{Advanced Gamification}:  Introduce more sophisticated gamification features, such as

          \begin{itemize}
            \item \textbf{Team vs. Team Challenges}: Allow different community groups to compete against each other.
            \item \textbf{Special Badges}: Award badges for specific achievements (e.g., "Plastic Warrior" for collecting 50kg of plastic).
            \item \textbf{Corporate Sponsorship}:  Partner with local businesses to sponsor prizes or challenges, providing a potential revenue stream.
          \end{itemize}

    \item \textbf{Data Dashboard for Authorities}:  Develop a web-based dashboard for our municipal partners, providing them with real-time data visualizations of waste collection activities, heatmaps of litter hotspots, and analytics on waste composition.
  \end{itemize}



  \section{Long-Term Vision (2+ Years)}

  \begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{National and Regional Scalingm}: Expand the Ayang platform to other cities and, eventually, other countries in the region, adapting the AI model for each new locality.

    \item \textbf{Integration with the Circular Economy}: Forge partnerships with manufacturers and recycling plants to create a closed-loop system. The data from Ayang could help predict the supply of recyclable materials, making the recycling process more efficient
    \item \textbf{Policy Advocacy}: Use the accumulated data to advocate for better waste management policies, such as extended producer responsibility (EPR) laws or the construction of new recycling facilities.

  \end{itemize}

  By following this strategic roadmap, Ayang can evolve from an innovative application into a cornerstone of a cleaner, more engaged, and environmentally conscious community across Africa.
